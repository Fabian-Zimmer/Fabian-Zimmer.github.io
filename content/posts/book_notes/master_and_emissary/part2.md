This article is about part 1 of the book [The Master and His Emissary](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/6968772-the-master-and-his-emissary) by Iain McGilchrist. In part 1, Iain gives us a comprehensive overview of the anatomy of our brain, what cognitive functions and abilities are associated with which hemisphere, and how the hemispheres each see and interact with the self, the world and subjective experience. Due to their frequent use, right hemisphere is abbreviated as RH and left hemisphere as LH.
